How We Work

Our 4-Stage Manufacturing Process

Every batch passes through four strict stages of formulation, validation, sterile packaging, and regulatory clearance.

01

Sourcing & R&D

Stringent qualification of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and primary packaging materials.

02

Sterile Processing

Automated formulation compounding and sterile pre-filled syringe (PFS) filling in Grade A cleanrooms.

03

Quality Control

Analytical assays, endotoxin checks, sterility tests, and stability validation for 100% batch compliance.

04

Packaging & Dispatch

Tamper-evident secondary packaging, cold-chain monitoring, and global logistics distribution.
