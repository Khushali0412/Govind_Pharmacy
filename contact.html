Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding contract manufacturing, MOQ, lead times, and regulatory support.

What do your two facilities offer?
Govind Remedies runs two facilities: Finished Dosage Formulation and Prefilled Syringe Technology. Please contact our team with your product, strength and pack-size requirements to discuss supply options.
What quality certifications does your facility hold?
Govind Remedies is an ISO certified company with a Good Manufacturing Practice certificate. Empty syringes are made under a medical device manufacturing licence from CDSCO and ISO 13485 certification. Please contact info@govindremedies.com for certificates and documentation.
How do I request technical documentation or samples?
Send your request through the contact form above, or write to Exports@govindremedies.com (export) or domesticsales@govindremedies.com (domestic).
Which markets do you supply?
Products are supplied to domestic and export markets. Each product page lists the strengths, pack sizes and market for that presentation.
