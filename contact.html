Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding contract manufacturing, MOQ, lead times, and regulatory support.

Do you offer contract manufacturing (CMO / CDMO) services?
Yes. We specialize in third-party and contract manufacturing for a wide range of pharmaceutical formulations, including sterile Pre-Filled Syringes (PFS), liquid injectables, and specialty ampoules. We accommodate custom batch sizes and customer-specific branding.
What quality certifications does your facility hold?
Govind Remedies operates under strict WHO-GMP guidelines with Grade A cleanroom isolators and 100% automated optical inspection systems. Our facilities undergo regular audits to ensure global pharmacopeial compliance (IP / BP / USP).
How can I request CTD / eCTD Dossiers for international registration?
Complete CTD / eCTD Dossiers (Modules 1 through 5), stability study data, and Certificate of Analysis (CoA) can be requested through our business development desk using the contact form above.
What is the typical commercial lead time for bulk supply?
Standard catalog formulations typically ship within 3 to 5 weeks. For custom CMO batches requiring specialized packaging or international cold-chain validation, lead times are approximately 6 to 8 weeks.
