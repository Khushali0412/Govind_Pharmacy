Advance Sterile Drug Delivery

Two dedicated facilities for advance sterile drug delivery: Finished Dosage Formulation and Prefilled Syringe Technology — manufactured under ISO and GMP certification.

Our Technical Capabilities

Finished Dosage Formulations

Sterile injectable formulations spanning anticoagulants, vitamins, critical care, pain management and more.

Prefilled Syringe Technology

Advanced prefilled syringes with staked-needle (SN) and Luer-tip (LT) presentations for controlled administration.

Empty Glass & COC/COP Syringes

Bonded / fixed-needle syringes with non-detachable cannula, from 0.5 ml to 50 ml.

Cartridges & Contrast Media

3 ml glass and COC cartridges plus 150 ml PC and COC contrast-media syringes.
