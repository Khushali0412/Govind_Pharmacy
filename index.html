Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our facilities, certifications, syringe range and supply.

What do your two facilities offer?
Finished Dosage Formulation for sterile injectables, and Prefilled Syringe Technology for ready-to-administer syringes. We also supply empty glass and COC / COP syringes and cartridges.
What quality certifications do you hold?
Govind Remedies is an ISO certified company with a Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certificate. Empty syringe manufacturing is supported by a medical device manufacturing licence from CDSCO and ISO 13485 certification.
What do SN and LT mean in the product list?
SN refers to a staked (fixed) needle syringe and LT to a Luer-tip syringe. Each product page shows the pack size for every strength.
What empty syringes do you offer?
Glass (0.5 to 10 ml), COC / COP (1 to 50 ml), 3 ml glass and COC cartridges, and 150 ml PC and COC contrast-media syringes, with bonded needle and non-detachable cannula.
Do you supply export and domestic markets?
Yes. Contact Exports@govindremedies.com for export or domesticsales@govindremedies.com for domestic supply.
