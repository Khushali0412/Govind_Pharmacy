Clinical Pharmacology & Mechanism

Enoxaparin binds to antithrombin III (ATIII) with high affinity, creating an irreversible conformational change that accelerates the inhibition of factor Xa and factor IIa (thrombin). With an anti-Xa to anti-IIa ratio of ~3.8:1, it achieves potent antithrombotic efficacy with minimized hemorrhagic risk.
